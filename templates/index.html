How To Play

Answer Questions

Python selects questions based on your topic and difficulty.

Correct Answer

Answer correctly and your rocket moves forward.

Wrong Answer

A wormhole sends your rocket backwards.

Earn Points

Correct answers, streaks and special squares give you points.

Complete Your Mission

Reach square 100 and complete your learning mission.

About Superknova

Superknova is an educational game that combines quiz-based learning with a space-themed board game.

Python is the brain of Superknova. The frontend is responsible primarily for displaying the game and its animations.
